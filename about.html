Core Concept

How Child & Teacher Interact

🧒 CHILD
1Shows ISL hand sign to camera
2AI detects gesture in 0.7 seconds
3Confidence scored 0–100%
4Word appears in chat bubble
5TTS speaks the word aloud
⇄
👩‍🏫 TEACHER
1Reads child's signed word instantly
2Types a reply or clicks Quick Reply
3Reply appears in chat
4TTS speaks reply aloud to child
5Child hears the teacher's voice
No speech from child · No special hardware · Just a camera & browser · Works 100% offline
What We Built

Features

🤲
REAL-TIME ISL DETECTION

MediaPipe Hands tracks 21 landmarks per hand at 30+ FPS. Custom gesture classifier maps hand geometry to ISL signs. 0.7s hold threshold — comfortable for children.

AI · MEDIAPIPE
📊
AI CONFIDENCE SCORING

Live circular SVG gauge shows 0–100% confidence per frame. Color-coded: ✓ Excellent (green) · ✓ Detected (cyan) · ⚡ Adjusting (amber). Never shows failure.

AI · REAL-TIME
🧠
NEURAL NET VISUALIZER

Animated SVG neural network with nodes and edges that light up proportionally to gesture confidence. Visual proof of AI processing for hackathon demos.

VISUALIZATION
💬
TWO-WAY CHAT

Child's signed word → cyan bubble on right. Teacher's reply → purple bubble on left. Every message timestamped. ▶ Hear Again on every teacher bubble.

CHAT · UX
🔊
TEXT-TO-SPEECH ENGINE

Web Speech API — 100% on-device. Prefers Indian English (en-IN) voice. Reads signed words aloud AND teacher replies. Auto-unlocks browser audio on first tap.

TTS · WEB SPEECH
📝
SESSION TRANSCRIPT

Rolling transcript of all signed words. Read Aloud button speaks the full sentence. Export downloads a .txt session log with timestamps + confidence scores.

ANALYTICS · EXPORT
🎭
DEMO MODE

No camera needed. Signs cycle automatically every 4 seconds showing the full AI flow. Perfect for presentations and hackathon demos when camera access is blocked.

DEMO · FALLBACK
🌸
CHILD-FIRST SAFETY

Pause → breathing calm overlay. Never red X or failure states. Auto-encouragement every 3 signs. Camera never auto-starts. Child is always in control.

A11Y · INCLUSIVE

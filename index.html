How It Works

Simple for children. Powerful for clinicians.

1
👤
Set Up a Profile
Create a child profile with preferences. The AI immediately begins personalizing rewards to their interests.
2
🏝️
Choose a Festival World
Each world is themed around an Indian festival with a unique therapy skill — Diwali, Holi, Onam, Monsoon, or the Global Plaza.

🤲
Sign & Speak
Children communicate with gestures via camera. Signs are converted to text and sent to their teacher in real time.

Our UCB-1 bandit engine selects culturally relevant rewards — dance videos, folk songs, animal clips — personalized per session.
